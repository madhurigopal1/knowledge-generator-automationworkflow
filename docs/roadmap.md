# Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP

- brief intake and validation
- entity matching and industry classification
- proposed table of contents
- source allowlist and retrieval manager
- evidence-backed section generation
- internal placeholder handling
- branded DOCX export
- human review workflow

## Phase 2: High-confidence refresh

- source freshness detection
- stale claim detection
- diffing between versions
- selective re-generation of sections
- PDF output and executive-ready export

## Phase 3: Enterprise readyness

- SSO and RBAC
- internal knowledge connectors
- multi-brand templates
- controlled legal review workflows
- broader source governance and audit trails

## Phase 4: Scale and optimization

- analytics and performance dashboards
- template customization by industry
- workflow automation for recurring client types
- advanced summarization and relationship extraction
